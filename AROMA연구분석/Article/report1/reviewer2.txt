Comments and Suggestions for Authors
This paper proposes a data-driven placement framework that automates the handcrafted defect-background compatibility matrix. However, several issues remain; if properly addressed, the manuscript could be considered for publication in Applied Sciences.

1. The paper repeatedly claims to be "free of manual tuning" and "without hand-set constants," yet the hyperparameters in Equations (2) and (4) are manually set.
2. Only YOLOv8n is used as the downstream detector; it is recommended to supplement the experiments with more recent mainstream detectors.
3. Fixed thresholds are used for defect subtype classification without providing a threshold sensitivity analysis.
4. The CCI formula lists only three components, whereas the text explicitly states "four normalized components."
5. Quantitative results for Shannon entropy and the Gini coefficient are not reported.
6. The keywords include "ControlNet" and "Stable Diffusion," despite neither technique being used in the proposed method.
7. The references lack relevant works published within the last three years.
