##4.4. Ablation Study — Stage-wise Contribution of the Placement Pipeline (Severstal)

The placement pipeline of Section 3.2.4 makes three sequential decisions per synthetic sample: (i) defect ROI selection by morphology–context compatibility, (ii) clean-background assignment by fitness ranking, and (iii) intra-image site resolution by ring-based context scoring. To attribute the Severstal gain of Table 8 to these stages, a leave-one-out ablation replaces exactly one stage with its random counterpart while keeping the other two intact: ROI-random (random crop selection; AROMA background and site), Background-random (AROMA crops; randomly assigned backgrounds), and Site-random (AROMA crops and backgrounds; uniformly random paste position). One coupling is inherent and reported as such: randomly assigned backgrounds carry no ring statistics, so Background-random also reverts site resolution to random — it removes stages (ii) and (iii) jointly rather than (ii) alone. All ablation arms were trained under the exact protocol of Table 8 (YOLOv8n, identical hyperparameters, n = 3 seeds) with the synthetic budget equalized across arms (2,534 images, matching the real training-set size); the Baseline and Random rows are carried over from Table 8 for reference.

**Table 11.** Leave-one-out ablation of the AROMA placement pipeline on Severstal (YOLOv8n mAP@0.5, mean ± std, n = 3 seeds). A/R denote the AROMA/random variant of each stage (ROI selection · background assignment · site resolution).

| Method | Stages (ROI·BG·Site) | mAP@0.5 | vs. AROMA (full) |
|--------|----------------------|---------|------------------|
| AROMA (full) | A·A·A | 0.5197 ± 0.0152 | — |
| Random | R·R·R | 0.5065 ± 0.0171 | −1.32 pp |
| Baseline | — (real only) | 0.5033 ± 0.0221 | −1.64 pp |
| Background-random | A·R·R | 0.4997 ± 0.0298 | −2.00 pp |
| Site-random | A·A·R | 0.4991 ± 0.0311 | −2.06 pp |
| ROI-random | R·A·A | 0.4817 ± 0.0091 | −3.80 pp |

**Every partial variant falls below the full pipeline.** Removing ROI selection costs −3.80 pp (per-seed deficits of 4.31/4.18/2.92 pp — the same sign in all three seeds, with the smallest deficit still 2.9 pp), removing site resolution costs −2.06 pp (same sign in 3/3 seeds), and removing background assignment, with its cascaded site loss, costs −2.00 pp (2/3 seeds; one seed ties). At n = 3 these are directional rather than inferential results, but the ROI-selection deficit exceeds seed-level variation in every seed.

**The stage contributions are not additive; they are complementary.** Under an additive model the all-random configuration — every stage removed — would be the weakest synthetic arm. Instead, Random is the strongest arm after the full pipeline (0.5065), and every single-stage ablation lands below it and below the real-only Baseline. Most tellingly, ROI-random trails Random in all three seeds (−3.53/−2.66/−1.27 pp): random crops placed by AROMA underperform random crops placed randomly. The mechanism follows from the background-pool observation of Section 4.3: similarity-ranked background assignment concentrates placements onto a reduced background pool, which pays off only when the crops themselves are compatibility-selected; combined with random crops, the concentration inherits the diversity restriction without the compatibility benefit. Likewise, Background-random and Site-random converge (0.4997 vs. 0.4991): once the paste position is random, which clean background was selected no longer matters — the value of background fitness is realized through the site-scoring stage that consumes it.

**Implication.** The ablation therefore does not decompose the Table 8 gain into per-stage increments; it shows the gain to be a property of the coherent three-stage chain. Each stage conditions the next — compatibility-selected crops justify the background concentration, and background fitness is cashed in by site resolution — so removing any single stage breaks the chain and yields synthetic data that is worse than uniformly random augmentation, and worse than no augmentation at all. This directly motivates the integrated design of Section 3.2.4 over any reduced variant of it.
