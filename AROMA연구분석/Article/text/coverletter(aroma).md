Thank you for considering our manuscript entitled, "AROMA: Adaptive ROI-based Morphology-Aware Augmentation" for submission to Applied Sciences (Intelligent Automation Technologies for Industry 4.0 - Special Issue).

Surface defect detection is essential for quality assurance in manufacturing, yet deep learning–based inspection systems often suffer from limited training data and class imbalance. To address these challenges, we propose AROMA, Adaptive ROI-based Morphology-Aware Augmentation that automatically generates context-consistent defect samples across heterogeneous industrial datasets.

The main contributions of this work are:

* Automatic compatibility modeling from patch-level defect–background co-occurrence statistics, eliminating handcrafted, per-dataset compatibility rules.
* A characterization of when context-aware ROI selection is beneficial through two dataset-level complexity indices (MCI and CCI).
* Comprehensive evaluation on five heterogeneous industrial datasets.

Experimental results demonstrate that AROMA provides an effective and generalizable framework for industrial defect data augmentation.
