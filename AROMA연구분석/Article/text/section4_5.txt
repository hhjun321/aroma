##4.5. Sensitivity of the Tertile Partition Boundaries

The quantities that drive AROMA's placement decisions are estimated from each dataset's own statistics (Sections 3.2.2–3.2.4): the morphology clusters by BIC-selected Gaussian mixture, the compatibility model from observed context distributions, and the ROI score of Eq. (2) as an unweighted sum of two such data-derived quantities, introducing no coefficients of its own. The one dataset-independent convention that remains is the pair of percentile positions defining the tertile partitions (P33/P66). This section verifies that this convention does not function as a hidden tuning knob.

**Tertile-boundary sensitivity (P33/P66).** The subtype labels of Section 3.2.3 serve interpretation and reporting; the ROI ranking of Eq. (2) does not consume them, so a boundary shift cannot alter which placements are generated. The remaining question is whether the labels themselves are stable. The percentile positions were perturbed by ±5 percentile points, individually and jointly (P33 → 28–38, P66 → 61–71, eight perturbed configurations per dataset), with the per-dataset degeneracy safeguard of Section 3.2.3 applied unchanged at the perturbed positions. The response is proportional and exhibits no instability cliff: a single-boundary shift relabels 3.1–9.1% of defects and a joint shift relabels 7.7–14.5% (Table 12) — in each case the order of the distribution mass moved by the shift itself, not an amplification of it.

**Table 12.** Stability of the subtype labels under tertile-boundary perturbation (P33/P66 shifted by ±5 percentile points). Ranges over the perturbed configurations of each group.

| Perturbation | Subtype reassignment |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Single boundary ±5 pt | 3.1–9.1% |
| Both boundaries ±5 pt | 7.7–14.5% |

Together with the ablation of Section 4.4, these results locate the source of AROMA's effect precisely: downstream gains require the presence of the context-compatibility mechanism — removing a placement stage changes the outcome, and replacing the compatibility-scored selection with a fully random one costs 3.8 pp of mAP on Severstal (Table 11) — while the one remaining fixed convention, the tertile boundary positions, can be perturbed without consequence for placement and with only proportional movement in the reported labels.
